About Microsoft Skills Navigator

Microsoft Skills Navigator by atozazure is a comprehensive, interactive roadmap companion for IT professionals navigating Microsoft's certification and skilling landscape. From foundational cloud literacy to expert-level architecture and AI engineering, the platform provides visual prerequisite roadmaps, role-based career timelines, exam cost estimations, and renewal alerts to empower your technical learning journey.
